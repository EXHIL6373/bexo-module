1. Document Overview
Purpose: This document defines the functional requirements and feature structure for the BEXO Admin Dashboard.
The Admin Dashboard is the company-level control center for managing the complete BEXO college ecosystem.
Scope: The documentation covers administrative capabilities for colleges, agreements, access, users, students,
provisioning, services, quotas, requests, payments/finance indicators, notifications, reports, audit/security, system
health, search, profile and settings. Technical architecture, database design, APIs, infrastructure and implementation
details are intentionally outside this document.
Product Principle: BEXO controls the platform centrally, while approved colleges operate only within the scope
granted to them.
2. Admin Dashboard Objectives
Primary Objective: Provide authorized BEXO administrators with centralized visibility and control over colleges,
users, students, agreements, services, access, provisioning and operational events.
Secondary Objectives: Reduce operational effort; make important exceptions visible; provide controlled approval
and access workflows; preserve historical activity; and keep company-level controls authoritative over college-level
operations.
3. User Roles and Administrative Scope
Company Administrators: Manage the overall BEXO platform and company-wide operations.
Company Operators: Perform assigned operational tasks within their approved responsibilities and permissions.
College Administrators: Represent an approved college and operate only within the college scope assigned by
BEXO.
Scope Rule: Company administrators have platform-level authority. College users must not receive information or
actions outside their approved college scope.
4. Admin Information Architecture
Main Navigation: Dashboard; Approval Center; Colleges; MOU / Agreements; Payments & Finance; Access Control;
Users & Roles; Students; Bulk Provisioning; Services & Entitlements; Requests & Approvals; Notifications; Reports &
Analytics; Audit & Security; System Health; Global Search; Profile & Settings.
Navigation Principle: The sidebar should prioritize high-frequency operational areas while keeping governance,
reporting and security functions clearly separated.
5. Executive Dashboard
Purpose: Provide an overall view of the platform's current operating status.
Key Information: College activity including active, pending, suspended and agreement-expiry situations;
student-related activity; recent provisioning activity; quota limits; agreement or payment due items; failed jobs; and
security alerts.
Quick Actions: Provide direct access to important operational areas such as pending approvals, colleges requiring
attention, failed provisioning jobs and expiring agreements.
6. College Management
Purpose: Manage the complete lifecycle of colleges connected to BEXO.

College List: Administrators can view all colleges, search college records, review status and operational information,
and access college-specific details.
College Actions: Create/onboard a college after the required approval process; update authorized college
information; view access, services and student activity; change lifecycle state; and open the college-specific
workspace.
College Lifecycle: Support states such as Pending, Active and Suspended. The platform must preserve student
profiles and historical information when a college is suspended.
College Profile: Maintain contact information, operational status, associated staff and administrators, student counts,
service usage, agreement information, quota information and assigned services.
7. College Onboarding Workflow
Step 1: Create or review the college record.
Step 2: Complete required college onboarding information.
Step 3: Review the applicable MOU/agreement.
Step 4: Configure approved quota and services.
Step 5: Provide controlled access to authorized college staff.
Step 6: Monitor the college from the central dashboard.
Expected Outcome: A college becomes operational only after the required approval, agreement, service, quota and
access controls are satisfied.
8. MOU / Agreement Management
Purpose: Maintain the agreement or MOU associated with a college.
Requirements: View agreement status and validity period; track start and end dates; retain the related agreement
document reference; identify agreements approaching expiry; support lifecycle actions; and make agreement status
visible when it affects college access or services.
Attention State: Agreements approaching expiry should appear as administrative attention items.
9. Access Control Center
Purpose: Control which colleges and users are allowed to access BEXO services.
Requirements: Manage access at the approved college scope; support department-level and student-limit
restrictions where applicable; approve, change, suspend or revoke access; expose clear access states such as
Active, Pending and Suspended; and prevent access outside the approved scope.
Authority Rule: College-side changes must not bypass company-level access controls.
10. Users, Admins and Roles
Company Users: Manage company-side users and operators, assign responsibilities and permissions, control
operator scope, review user status and revoke or change permissions when required.
College Users: Manage or oversee college administrators and their relationship to the assigned college.
Permission Principle: Administrative actions should be limited by role and approved scope.
11. Student Management
Purpose: Provide company-wide visibility and administration of students associated with colleges.

Student Information: Name, college, department, roll/enrollment number, email where available, status and eligible
service access.
Actions: Search and review student records; review service access; inspect provisioning history; and perform
permitted access revocation.
Scope: Admin users may view and administer student information across colleges according to their permissions.
12. Bulk Student Provisioning
Purpose: Support large-scale student access operations without manual processing of every student.
Requirements: Review provisioning activity across colleges; monitor batches and progress; view successful, failed
and partially completed records; review validation results and errors; and track provisioning jobs from creation
through completion.
Job States: Queued, Processing, Completed, Partially Completed, Failed and Cancelled.
13. Services & Entitlements
Services: Manage services available through the platform and assign services to approved colleges.
College Service Status: Administrators must be able to see whether a service is active for a college and control
availability according to college status and approved policies.
Student Entitlements: Manage or oversee student-level entitlements created through college access.
Access Principle: Student access is based on an active entitlement rather than a permanent unrestricted
free-access flag.
14. College Suspension & Reactivation
Suspension: Administrators can suspend a college when required by company policy or agreement status.
Impact: Suspended college services must no longer be treated as active, while student accounts, profiles and
historical information remain preserved.
Reactivation: Administrators can reactivate a college when eligibility is restored. Eligible student access becomes
effective again according to applicable service and entitlement rules.
Operational Review: The administrator should be able to review the impact of suspension and related notifications.
15. Requests & Approvals
Purpose: Provide controlled handling of operational requests that require company action.
Requirements: Review pending requests; approve or reject eligible requests; show pending approval items; preserve
traceable approval activity; and prevent unrestricted college-side changes.
States: Pending, Approved and Rejected.
16. Payments & Finance Indicators
Purpose: Provide visibility into payment or finance-related indicators when connected to the applicable business
process.
Requirements: Show payment-related attention items; provide payment status where it affects college operations or
agreements; and provide centralized visibility for relevant financial events.
Scope Note: This document defines visibility and operational indicators, not a complete accounting system.

17. Notifications Center
Purpose: Centralize operational notifications for administrators.
Events: Student access granted, provisioning completion, quota warnings, agreement expiry, college suspension
and other important operational events.
Requirements: View notification history, track sent notifications and identify items requiring operational follow-up.
18. Reports & Analytics
Purpose: Support management decisions using centralized platform information.
Reports: Operational summaries across colleges and students; provisioning volumes and outcomes; service usage;
college activity; trends and attention areas.
Scope: Admin reporting is company-wide, subject to role permissions.
19. Audit & Security Center
Purpose: Provide historical accountability for important administrative actions.
Audit Data: Who performed an action, what was changed and when it occurred.
Sensitive Events: College lifecycle changes, role changes, quota changes, student provisioning, service changes
and security actions.
Outcome: Administrators can review historical activity for accountability and operational review.
20. System Health & Operations
Purpose: Surface important operational conditions and failures.
Monitoring Areas: Failed provisioning activity, repeated operational problems, notification and job-related issues,
and security or authentication alerts.
Outcome: Administrators can quickly identify areas requiring attention.
21. Global Search
Purpose: Provide a central search experience for important platform records.
Search Scope: Colleges, students, administrators and operational records.
Benefit: Reduce navigation effort and make important identifiers easier to locate.
22. Admin Profile & Settings
Profile: Manage administrator profile information and review account/access information.
Settings: Provide applicable settings and preferences and controlled platform configuration where authorized.
23. Core Admin Workflows
College Onboarding: Dashboard → Create/Review College → Complete Information → Review MOU → Configure
Quota & Services → Add Authorized Staff → Approve → Monitor.
Suspend / Reactivate: Open College → Review Status/Agreement/Services → Suspend → Services become
inactive while history is preserved → Review impact → Reactivate when eligible.
Provisioning Oversight: Open Provisioning → Select College/Job → Review progress → Inspect successful/failed
records → Review validation errors → Follow up.

24. Admin vs College Dashboard Boundary
Admin: Company-wide control over colleges, agreements, users, students, provisioning, services, entitlements,
quotas, suspension, requests, notifications, reports, audit and settings.
College: Single-college operation including student management, student access provisioning, assigned services,
college users, requests, agreement view, notifications, college-scoped reporting and settings.
Security Principle: College users see only information and actions applicable to their approved college scope.
25. Product Experience Principles
Control: The Admin Dashboard is designed for control, visibility, governance and operational management.
Clarity: Important actions should provide clear status, confirmation and error information.
Authority: Company-level controls remain authoritative over college-level operations.
Scope: College users must not see or perform actions outside their approved scope.
Entitlement: Student access is a controlled entitlement.
Preservation: Suspension affects access while preserving student records and operational history.
Scale: Large student operations should be easy to start and monitor.
Communication: Notifications should keep administrators and college staff informed of actions requiring attention.
26. Functional Requirement Checklist
Dashboard: Executive overview, attention items, college activity, student activity, provisioning activity.
Colleges: List, search, create/onboard, update, lifecycle management, college workspace.
Agreements: Status, validity, dates, document reference, expiry monitoring.
Access: Approve/change/suspend/revoke access, scope restrictions.
Users: Company users, operators, college administrators, roles and permissions.
Students: Search, status, college, department, roll/enrollment, email, access and history.
Provisioning: Batches, progress, successful/failed/partial records, validation errors.
Services: Service catalog, college assignments, active status, entitlements.
Requests: Pending/approved/rejected workflows and traceability.
Payments: Payment/finance indicators and attention items.
Notifications: Operational notifications and history.
Reports: Cross-college summaries, provisioning, service usage and trends.
Audit: Who/what/when history and sensitive event tracking.
System Health: Failed jobs, notifications, operational and security alerts.
Search: Cross-platform search.
Settings: Profile, access information and authorized configuration.
